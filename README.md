## A few reasons why

🗣️ **Helping a person speak again.** Researchers combined AI with a brain implant to synthesize speech for a participant with severe paralysis. This was a clinical research result. [Read the research →](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-025-01905-6) · [University explainer](https://www.universityofcalifornia.edu/news/brain-voice-neuroprosthesis-restores-naturalistic-speech)

📜 **Reading history without unrolling it.** AI helped researchers reveal writing inside a carbonized Herculaneum scroll through scans and virtual unwrapping. Scholars interpreted the recovered text. [See the discovery →](https://scrollprize.org/grandprize)

🧬 **Sharing over 200 million protein structure predictions.** AlphaFold and EMBL-EBI made a vast collection of predictions freely available to researchers. [Explore the project →](https://deepmind.google/science/alphafold/)
