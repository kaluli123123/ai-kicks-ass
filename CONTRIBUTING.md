# Share what AI helped you do

A small, real accomplishment belongs here just as much as a research breakthrough.

## Share a win

Use the [submission form](https://github.com/kaluli123123/ai-kicks-ass/issues/new?template=share-a-win.yml). Tell us what you made or accomplished, which AI system helped, what you did yourself, and where someone can inspect the result. Include the model or tool version when you know it.

Submissions in any supported language are welcome. A public demo, repository, paper, or documented example is useful evidence. If your account cannot be independently checked, say so; firsthand stories are welcome but must be identified as such. Do not upload private conversations or other people's personal data to demonstrate a claim.

Submissions are suggestions. A maintainer reviews the evidence and wording before featuring a result. An open issue is not an endorsement. Keep README additions brief and link directly to a supporting source. Give people credit and retain material limits, including research-only settings or provider-reported numbers.

## Improve the project

See [language editions and review status](LANGUAGES.md). Translate meaning rather than strengthening claims. Preserve dates, quantities, source links, human credit, and material limitations. Native-speaker review is welcome. If a source claim changes, update its translations or label affected editions as outdated.

Corrections, clearer wording, and accessibility improvements are welcome. Use the [correction form](https://github.com/kaluli123123/ai-kicks-ass/issues/new?template=correction.yml) or submit a small pull request explaining the change. There is no build step. Preview Markdown, verify links, and check any artwork in light and dark surroundings before proposing it.

Keep discussion about results and evidence. No personal attacks, spam, or unsupported claims. We may shorten, correct, or remove featured entries when the evidence changes.

## Reuse and attribution

Contribute original text and artwork you can license under this repository's [MIT License](LICENSE). Link to outside articles, photographs, papers, and videos instead of copying them. Their authors retain their rights; inclusion does not imply affiliation or endorsement.
